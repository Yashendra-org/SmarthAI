Disclaimer & Transparency Notice

Independent Platform - Not a Government Service

Policy Portal is an independent platform built by Team 12. It is NOT operated, endorsed, or officially affiliated with the Government of India, any state government, or any public department.

We do not collect application fees, process government claims, issue cards, or approve benefits. All applications must be submitted directly through the official department links provided on each policy page.

Accuracy of Summaries

Our summaries, eligibility checklists, and document guides are created for informational convenience in plain language. Government guidelines, rules, and deadlines change frequently.

Always verify the latest rules and eligibility criteria on the official government website before applying or making financial commitments.

Application Numbers & Indicators

Numbers shown for "people who clicked Apply" reflect outbound link clicks from this portal, and "people who say they applied" represent self-reported survey answers from citizens. They are not official applicant counts. Official application data is held solely by respective government departments.

Sentiment Analysis & Demo Data

  • Sentiment charts are generated using automated natural language processing models (Lexicon & Transformer engines) on public feedback.
  • While we strive for high accuracy on English and Hinglish comments, machine learning models may occasionally misinterpret sarcasm, nuances, or mixed vocabulary.
  • Any demonstrative seed feedback loaded during testing is explicitly flagged with an Includes sample data for demo badge and is never represented as genuine citizen opinion.
