How sentiment is calculated

Each comment is read by a computer program that labels it positive, negative or neutral, and gives a confidence score.

  • The program works on English and Hinglish (Hindi written in English letters).
  • It can be wrong, especially with sarcasm, very short comments and mixed languages.
  • Comments flagged as spam are left out of every chart.
  • A chart appears only after enough responses, so a few comments cannot misrepresent a policy.
  • Application numbers show clicks on the Apply button and what respondents tell us. They are not official applicant counts.

Demo data is always labelled as sample data.
