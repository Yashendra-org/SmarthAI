# Demo Script (about 5 minutes)

1. **Problem (30 s):** policies are hard to find and understand, and public opinion is hard to measure.
2. **Home page:** show "Browse by who you are" and search for a policy by need, not by name.
3. **Policy page:** plain summary, documents needed, last verified date, disclaimer, official-site button.
4. **Feedback:** submit one English and one Hinglish comment.
5. **Sentiment:** open /admin/ and show the label and confidence given to each comment.
6. **Public chart:** show the chart on a policy with sample data and point out the "sample data" label.
7. **Dashboard:** per-policy stats, recent comments, spam flags.
8. **Honest limits:** sarcasm and mixed Hinglish can fool the model, application numbers are clicks and self-reports, and the portal is independent of the government.
9. **Future:** government roles with an approval workflow, deployment, Hindi support.
